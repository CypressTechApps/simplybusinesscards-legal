Support

Need help with Simply Business Cards? Email support@cypresstech.net (or use Settings → Send feedback in the app) and include your device model and Android version if the question is about a bug. You can also reach us through the contact form at tech.cypress.company.

Common questions

Where are my cards stored?

Only on your device, in the app's private storage. Nothing is uploaded. Uninstalling the app deletes all cards, so use Settings → Back up cards first.

Why does it ask for camera permission?

The app runs the camera itself so it can find the card, straighten it and crop it. It asks the first time you open the scanner — never at launch — and you can decline and still import a picture you already have.

A detail was read wrong.

Fix it on the Check details screen before you save, or open the card and tap the pencil; Edit photo there lets you re-crop or rotate the card. "Read card again" in the card's ⋮ menu fills in anything still blank.

How do I scan a stack of cards?

Bulk scanning is part of Premium. Choose Bulk at the top of the camera and photograph each card in turn; every photo becomes its own card. The free version scans one card at a time.

How do I move my cards to a new phone?

Settings → Back up cards saves one file wherever you choose. On the new phone, Settings → Restore from a backup adds those cards (cards already there are skipped).

How do I remove ads?

Open Settings and choose Get Premium — it's a one-time purchase, no subscription, and restores automatically on any device signed into your Google account.
