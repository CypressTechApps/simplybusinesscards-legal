The app

Simply Business Cards is a business-card scanning utility by Cypress Technology LLC. It photographs business cards with your device's camera, reads the contact details on-device, and stores the cards on your device so you can tag, search and share them. The free version is supported by a banner advertisement and scans one card at a time; a one-time Premium purchase adds bulk scanning and removes ads.

Your content

The cards you scan and the details on them stay on your device. You are responsible for how you use and share other people's contact information, and for complying with applicable laws. Because your cards exist only on your device, you are also responsible for your own backups (Settings → Back up cards) — if you delete the app or lose the device without a backup, we cannot recover your cards.
