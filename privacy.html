Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

The short version: Simply Business Cards reads and stores your business cards entirely on your device. We do not have servers, we do not have accounts, and we never see, upload, or collect your cards or the contact details on them. The only data processing beyond your device comes from the advertising that supports the free version — and a one-time purchase removes ads entirely.

What Simply Business Cards itself collects

Nothing. Card photos, the names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and notes read from them, and your tags are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Deleting a card deletes its photo and details; uninstalling the app deletes everything. The app has no account system and sends none of your content anywhere.

Camera and card photos

Scanning uses your device's camera, which the app operates directly. It asks for camera permission the first time you open the scanner — never at launch — and you can decline and still import a picture you already have.

Text recognition uses Google's ML Kit, which is built into the app and runs on your device. Like other Google SDKs, ML Kit may send Google anonymous usage and diagnostic information about how the recognizer performs — never your card photos or the text it reads.

Contacts and sharing

The app does not read your phone's contacts and does not ask for contacts permission. "Add to contacts" opens your phone's own contacts app with the details filled in, and you choose whether to save. Sharing a card (as a contact file, text, photo or QR code) only happens when you tap Share or show the QR code, and goes only where you send it.

Backups

"Back up cards" writes a single file to a location you choose with your phone's file picker (your phone, an SD card, a USB drive, or a cloud folder you pick). We never receive it. "Restore from a backup" reads a file you choose and adds its cards.

Advertising (free version only)

The free version shows a single banner ad served by Google AdMob. As with any mobile advertising SDK, Google may collect and process certain data to provide and secure ads, including approximate location, app interaction data, diagnostics, and device or other identifiers (such as the advertising ID). This data is used for analytics, advertising, and fraud prevention, and is transmitted encrypted. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in AdMob.

In the European Economic Area and the UK, you will be asked for consent before any ad is shown, and you can revisit your choice at any time via "Ad privacy options" in the app's Settings.

Removing ads: the one-time Premium purchase removes the banner and the advertising SDK is not started. Purchases are processed by Google Play; we receive no payment details.

Purchases

The optional one-time Premium purchase is handled entirely by Google Play Billing. We do not receive or store your name, card details, or billing address. Your entitlement is verified with Google Play.

Children

Simply Business Cards is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Because your cards exist only on your device (and in any backup file you chose to make), you are in full control: delete cards in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all data. We hold nothing to delete on our side.

Changes
